DialogReader

Hear the text in your games.

DialogReader watches your game and reads dialogue, item pickups and other on-screen text out loud, with a voice that runs on your PC.

1
Pick your game
Start the game first. DialogReader finds it.
2
Mark where text shows up
Draw boxes over subtitles, names and pop-ups.
3
Choose a key, a voice and a mode
Then play. The window can stay closed.
Get startedImport settings

Takes about two minutes. You can change everything later.
