Hear your games, not just read them

DialogReader watches the parts of the screen you choose and reads the text out loud as it appears. Set up a game once, then just play.

  1. 1Choose the game window
  2. 2Mark where the text shows up
  3. 3Pick a key and a voice
  4. 4Choose when it reads

Takes about 3 minutes. Everything runs on this PC.
